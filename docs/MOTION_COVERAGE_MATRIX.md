Coverage tests enumerate every marked element in every listed section; each must reach `complete` after entering the viewport, retain opacity 1 afterward and not restart during return scrolling. Test attachments record element counts for each section. Calm-motion regression tests separately verify near-edge fades, immediate fast-anchor fallback, initial spotlight alignment and continuous menu reversal. Before/after audits record 80–81 visible opacity resets per scenario before refinement and zero afterward.

Evidence and measurements are linked in `MOTION_SYSTEM.md`, `PROGRESS.md` and `QA_CHECKLIST.md`. Screenshots verify settled composition, while the recording shows timing; screenshots alone are not proof of motion quality. Headless Chromium and emulated CPU/network runs do not replace physical low-end Android/iOS, Safari or assistive-technology acceptance testing.
